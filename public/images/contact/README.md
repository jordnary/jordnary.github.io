# Contact artwork

## Airmail insignia

- Asset: `airmail-insignia.webp`
- Subject: 1923 U.S. Air Mail Insignia, 16¢ dark blue, Scott C5.
- Author: Bureau of Engraving and Printing; imaging credited to Gwillhickers.
- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1923_Dark_Blue_U.S._Air_Mail_Insignia_C5.jpg), citing the U.S. Post Office and Smithsonian National Postal Museum.
- Rights: the source identifies this U.S. federal government work as public domain in the United States (PD-USGov / Public Domain Mark).
- Treatment: removed the connected black scan background outside the perforations; preserved the stamp artwork and paper, resized to 240 × 204 pixels, and encoded as WebP without source metadata.
- Used as a decorative stamp on the Contact email card. The hero postcard uses its original geometric SVG stamp. Reviewed on 2026-10-10.
