# About image assets

## Outro paper

- `outro-paper.webp` is a 720 × 880 grayscale texture extracted from the empty right-hand paper area of the user-provided reference.
- The photographed shallow wrinkles retain their paired highlights and shadows. Isolated dark flecks are softened; only the sparse natural fiber marks remain.
- Boundary differences are removed for seamless tiling, with broad lighting drift gently reduced. Lossless WebP preserves the subtle relief without compression blotches.
- The texture matches the existing sage surface luminance. `luminosity` blending preserves its hue and chroma, and a fixed 640px tile width maintains the same fine wrinkle scale on desktop and mobile.
